# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Inqtrix.

Inqtrix is distributed under the Apache License, Version 2.0
(`Apache-2.0`).

By contributing to this project, you agree that your contributions are submitted
under `Apache-2.0`.

You confirm that you have the right to submit your contribution and that it does
not knowingly infringe third-party rights.

Please include a sign-off in each commit:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

See [docs/development/contributing.md](docs/development/contributing.md) for the
full development workflow.
